About Our Hotel Logo

This hotel logo represents comfort, quality, and premium hospitality services. The building icon in the center symbolizes accommodation and luxury stay experiences.

The circular background reflects global reach and continuous service, while the surrounding fork and spoon design represent hospitality, dining excellence, and complete guest satisfaction.

The bold red and blue color combination conveys trust, passion, professionalism, and warmth — key values that define our hotel brand.
